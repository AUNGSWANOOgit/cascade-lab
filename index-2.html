1Sequence

How the cascade method works
  1. Group. Cut the sequence into groups, left to right, so no cylinder letter appears twice in the same group. Use as few groups as you can.
  2. Group lines. Each group gets its own air line (G1, G2…). The cascade valves make sure only one line has air at a time. You need one cascade valve fewer than the number of groups.
  3. First move of a group takes its pilot air straight from that group's line.
  4. Every other move is triggered by the limit valve that confirms the move before it, for example a1 means “A is fully out”. That limit valve takes its air from the current group line.
  5. Last limit valve in a group switches the cascade to the next group. The last one of the whole cycle goes through START and switches back to Group I.
  6. At rest the last group line is live, so the circuit waits for START.

Notation: X+ extends cylinder X and X− retracts it. x1 is the limit valve pressed when X is fully out, x0 when X is fully in.

Shortcuts: Space holds START, S runs one step, Esc cancels a wire, Delete removes the selected wire, Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo and redo.
